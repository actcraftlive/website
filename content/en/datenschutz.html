2. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When you open the site, GitHub processes the data technically needed to deliver it, in particular:

  • your IP address,

According to GitHub, it uses this data to run the service securely and reliably, for example to fend off attacks, and also relies on service providers such as a content delivery network. Transfer to the USA: GitHub also processes data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is therefore covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR.

3. No cookies, no tracking, no third-party content

This site stores nothing on your device and reads nothing from it; consent under § 25 TDDDG is therefore not required. There are no analytics tools, no advertising, no social media plugins, no embedded videos or maps and no externally hosted fonts. A Content Security Policy technically prevents the site from loading content from other addresses.

Switching between English and German is an ordinary link to the other language version. Your choice is neither stored nor transmitted.

4. Links to other sites

The site links to the project on GitHub. Only when you click such a link does your browser connect to the other provider, whose privacy policy then applies. Your browser is instructed not to tell it which page you came from.

5. Contact by letter

If you get in touch by letter, your name, address and message are processed to answer your request (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

E-mails to the address above are technically received by the e-mail provider but are not read, answered or processed any further.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21). Please contact the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the federal state or member state where you live or work.

This policy is updated when the site or the legal situation changes.
